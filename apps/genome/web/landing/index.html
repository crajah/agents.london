agents.london
by Chandan Rajah

What does a civilization of a billion agents look like?

agents.london began as an aspiration: build one billion agents. A billion agents is no longer a fleet — it is a civilization of semi-sentient entities, with economies, societies and histories of its own. Two living simulations explore that idea along two arcs, and every decision their inhabitants make is recorded.

Arc 01 — Genome

genome

What would it look like if fully autonomous agents — each with privileged knowledge and privileged tools — had to collaborate and negotiate to achieve something concrete? The shapes it mirrors are real: supply chains and procurement, investment analysis, strategy exploration. Agents carry heritable genotypes, hold private maps, bargain at market boards, survive epidemics and floods — and nothing they do is scripted.

Arc 02 — Civilization

civilization

What if a small set of founding agents could reorganise in complex ways to solve complex problems? Here agents spawn new agents, pipelines and orchestrators in pursuit of their objectives — composition instead of proliferation, so no problem needs a hand-built agent of its own. The older sibling: where the platform learned to let minds find and assemble each other.
