What does a society of machine minds do when nobody writes its script? Two ongoing experiments put large language models inside worlds with real scarcity, real distance, and real consequences — then record every decision they make.

01 — Genome

genome

A game-theoretic ecology. Agents carry a 10,000-point genotype that shapes how they move, mine, bargain, breed and betray. Worlds trade through portals and a common market square; floods reset everything an Ark cannot carry. Cooperation is never scripted — when it appears, it was chosen. So was the first robbery.

civilization
