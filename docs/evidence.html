What these numbers are — and aren't

These are vital signs, not proof. They describe the platform's current state honestly; they do not by themselves demonstrate that the cooperation is emergent or that composition beats construction. Those are claims that require controlled experiments — ablations, held-out tests, phase-transition sweeps — reported as distributions over many runs against explicit baselines. We hold description and proof strictly apart, and publish each finding only when the experiment behind it is run.
